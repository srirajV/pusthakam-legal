PusthaKam – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

PusthaKam (the "App") is a reading app published by VIRAJAMANAM – A VARADA PUBLICATION ("A Varada Publication", "we", "us"), based in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and the choices you have. We are the data fiduciary for this information under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Google account details: your name, email address and Google account ID, received when you sign in with Google.To create and secure your account, to unlock content for you, and to restore your purchases on a new phone.
Reading activity: pages you bookmark. Your last-read page and your settings (language, theme, text size, reading mode) are stored on your phone.To show your bookmarks and let you continue where you stopped.
Printed-copy interest: the email address or phone number you enter in the "I'm interested" form.To know which books readers want in print, and to contact you about availability.
Notification token: an identifier for your phone, created when you allow notifications.To send you alerts about new stories and announcements. You can turn this off in Profile.
Purchase records (when paid books are available): which books you have unlocked, as confirmed by Google Play or the Apple App Store.To unlock books you bought. We do not receive or store your card or bank details.

The App does not contain advertising and does not sell your information. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

2. Your email on book pages

To discourage copying, the App shows a faint watermark containing your email address on book pages, and it blocks screenshots and screen recording where your phone allows. The watermark is shown on your own screen only.

3. Services that process data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in system (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Google provides Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notification delivery), and Google Play handles purchases on Android.
  • Expo provides the push-notification service that sends alerts to your phone.
  • Apple handles sign-in and purchases on iPhone, once the iPhone version is available.
  • Amazon: if you tap the link to buy a printed copy, you leave the App and Amazon's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive your Amazon order details.

These providers process data only to run these services. We share information with no one else except where the law requires.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information and bookmarks while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete these promptly. Printed-copy interest entries are deleted with your account. Records that the law requires us to keep (for example, tax records of paid sales, once paid books are available) are kept for the legally required period.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: open Profile, then "Delete my account". This removes your login, bookmarks, printed-copy interest and notification token. Purchases made through Google Play or Apple remain with your Google or Apple account.
  • Notifications: switch them off in Profile, or in your phone's settings.
  • Access, correction, erasure and grievances: you may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it, by writing to us at the address below. We will reply within a reasonable time, not later than 30 days.

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each reader can see only their own account data, and book pages are served through short-lived private links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Children

The App is intended for readers aged 18 and over, or for younger readers using it with a parent or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without that consent. If you believe a child has given us information without it, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App.
